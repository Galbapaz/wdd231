Resources & Grammar Tips

📘 Grammar Tips

1. Use Articles Correctly (a, an, the)

Use a before consonant sounds and an before vowel sounds. Use the when referring to something specific or already mentioned.

✅ "I saw a dog. The dog was friendly."

2. Subject-Verb Agreement

The verb must agree with the subject in number. Singular subjects take singular verbs; plural subjects take plural verbs.

✅ "She studies every day."  |  "They study every day."

3. Tense Consistency

Stay in the same tense throughout a sentence or paragraph unless the meaning requires a change in time.

✅ "She opened the book and started reading."
❌ "She opened the book and starts reading."

4. Prepositions of Time (in, on, at)

Use at for specific times, on for days and dates, and in for months, years, and longer periods.

✅ "at 5pm"  |  "on Monday"  |  "in January"

5. Countable vs Uncountable Nouns

Countable nouns can be pluralized (book → books). Uncountable nouns cannot (water, advice, information). Never use a/an with uncountable nouns.

✅ "Can you give me some advice?"
❌ "Can you give me an advice?"

🎯 Learning Strategies

📓

Keep a Vocabulary Journal

Write 5 new words every day with their definitions and an original example sentence you create yourself.

🔁

Spaced Repetition

Review new words after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, and 1 month. This technique dramatically improves long-term memory retention.

🎧

Listen and Repeat

Listen to English podcasts, songs, or videos and repeat what you hear out loud to improve pronunciation and natural fluency.

✍️

Write Every Day

Write a short paragraph in English every day using vocabulary words you have learned. Practice makes permanent.
