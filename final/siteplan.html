1. Site Name

English Spark

This name was chosen because it communicates energy, motivation, and the beginning of something — a "spark" of learning. It is short, memorable, and easy to associate with language learning. The word "English" makes the topic immediately clear, while "Spark" evokes curiosity and excitement, which is exactly the feeling this site aims to create in students who are beginning or continuing their English learning journey.

Possible domain: english-spark.com

2. Site Purpose

English Spark is a free, interactive English learning resource designed for students who are learning English as a second or foreign language. The site provides a vocabulary practice section where students can explore English words and phrases with definitions, example sentences, and difficulty levels. Students can filter vocabulary by category, save their favorite words using local storage, and click on any word card to open a modal with full word details and usage tips. The site also includes a resources page with curated grammar tips and learning strategies, as well as a contact form where students can suggest words or topics they want to learn.

3. Scenarios

These are questions a typical site visitor (an English learner) might ask when visiting English Spark:

"I am a beginner. Where can I find easy vocabulary words with examples to practice every day?"
"How can I save the words I want to study later so I can come back to them?"
"I want to know what the difference is between formal and informal English words. Does this site explain that?"
"Can I suggest a word or topic I need help with to the teacher who made this site?"

5. Typography

Two Google Fonts will be used to create a balance between elegance and readability:

Merriweather – Headings (h1, h2, h3)

A serif font that conveys trust, education, and professionalism. Used for all page titles and section headings.

Welcome to English Spark

Open Sans – Body Text & UI

A clean, modern sans-serif font that is highly legible on screens of all sizes. Used for paragraphs, labels, buttons, and navigation.

Learn new words every day. Build your vocabulary, practice with examples, and improve your English step by step.
